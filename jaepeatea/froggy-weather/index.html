Designed for Fun

We believe checking the weather shouldn't be a mundane task. Froggy Weather combines a highly polished design aesthetic with fluid animations that make interacting with meteorological data a genuinely joyful experience.

  • Dynamic Backgrounds: Watch the app background shift to match the time of day and current weather conditions.
  • Widget Support: Bring the frog directly to your home screen with beautifully crafted Android widgets.
  • Hyper-local Radar: Detailed weather information presented in a clean, uncluttered interface.
